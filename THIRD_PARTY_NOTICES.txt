1000 Genomes Phase 3
Source: https://www.internationalgenome.org/
Reuse statement: https://www.internationalgenome.org/IGSR_disclaimer/
The published Phase 3 data are available without embargo. Cite the 1000
Genomes Project Consortium, doi:10.1038/nature15393. Other IGSR collections
can have different terms; this workflow does not assume the same terms for
all IGSR resources. Raw reference genotypes are downloaded locally and are
not included in this repository.

The custom hierarchical model and simulation scripts are distinct from
the published Manc-COJO and MultiSuSiE implementations. Scientific citations
and software provenance are supplied in the research manuscript.
